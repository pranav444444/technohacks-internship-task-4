📊 Project Summary:
Developed a fully interactive Excel Dashboard to analyze and visualize Vrinda Store’s sales performance throughout 2022. This dashboard aims to help the business better understand customer behavior and identify key areas for improving sales strategies in 2023.

🔍 Key Insights Provided:

Comparative analysis of sales vs. orders using dynamic visuals.

Identification of the top-performing month by sales and order volume.

Gender-based purchase behavior: Men vs. Women.

Overview of various order status types in 2022.

Top 10 states contributing to total sales.

Visualization of the relationship between age & gender based on order count.

Channel-wise contribution: Which sales channel drove the most revenue.

Discovery of the highest-selling product category.

📌 Tools Used:

Microsoft Excel

Pivot Tables, Charts

Slicers, Filters

Data Cleaning & Duplicate Removal

Interactive Dashboard Design

💡 Outcome:
The dashboard enables business stakeholders to make data-driven decisions and plan effective sales and marketing strategies for the upcoming year by understanding purchasing trends and customer demographics.
